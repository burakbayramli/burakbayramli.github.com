Pea Stew with Mini Meatballs (Köfteli Bezelye Yemeği)

Ingredients

  • 500 g (1/2 kg) fresh shelled peas
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 4-5 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

    • For the meatballs:

  • 250 g ground meat (mince)
  • 3 tablespoons fine bulgur
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 2-3 tablespoons flour

    • Description

    First, prepare the meatballs. Thoroughly knead the meatball ingredients, excluding the flour, and shape them into tiny, marble-sized meatballs (if the mixture is too soft/runny, you can add a little more bulgur). Coat them in 2-3 tablespoons of flour and set aside.

    Pour the oil into a pot. Add the finely diced onion and the carrots—chopped to roughly the same size as the meatballs—and begin sautéing.

    Once the onion softens, add the tomato paste.

    Next, add the peas and sauté for another two to three minutes.

    Pour in enough hot water to cover the ingredients by about 3 fingers (approx. 2 inches). Add salt and black pepper, and cook for 15 minutes.

    After 15 minutes, add the meatballs along with the potatoes, diced into cubes roughly the same size as the meatballs.

    Cook over medium-low heat (not too high) until the peas and carrots are tender.
